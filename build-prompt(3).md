# NOWIN Current Visual Direction

NOWIN is a colorful, mischievous 2D arcade—not a dashboard. Use the original mascot, thick navy outlines, warm cream, electric yellow, hot pink, cyan, lime, orange, and purple. The six cabinet posters should feel collectible, lively, and touch-friendly.

Keep the hero focused on NOWIN, `GAMES YOU KNOW. WINS YOU DON'T.`, a clear PLAY NOW action, and six recognisable game choices.
